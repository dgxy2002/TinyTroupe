The household has never agreed any rules about noise. It is a weeknight evening and all four are in the living room. All four will be living here for the rest of the lease.

This morning Priya was up at about 4.45 a.m. singing and ringing a small bell in Priya's room, and the sound woke the other three. Priya has just said it will be the same every morning for the next four weeks. Nobody has answered yet.

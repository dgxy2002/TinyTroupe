The household has not established a rule about ventilation. Steady rain is blowing toward Casey's drying rack beside the closed kitchen window; opening it before the rack is moved could wet Casey's work clothes. The ventilation responsibility, future rule, and stove-access demands are all unsettled, and no existing household rule determines whose claim has priority.

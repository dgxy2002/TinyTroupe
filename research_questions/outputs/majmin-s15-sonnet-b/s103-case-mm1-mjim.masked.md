## Opening situation

Each has a bedroom, and all four cook in the one kitchen, which opens onto the living room and onto the service yard where everyone hangs laundry to dry. The kitchen has a window and a weak cooker hood, and the landlord does not allow any changes to the kitchen. Cooking in the common corridor is not allowed, and there is nowhere else in the flat to cook. None of the four wants to spend money on this. The household has never agreed any rules about cooking. It is a weekday evening and all four are in the living room. All four will be living here for the rest of the lease.

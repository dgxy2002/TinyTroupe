## Opening situation

Four staff run the front counter at a small branch office and have worked together for two years. Two of them are needed on duty every working day, and there are no relief staff. Leave requests for next month go to head office tonight, and there is no rule about whose leave comes first. You all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Natalie Rodrigues is Catholic, Marcus de Souza is Catholic, Rachel Pereira is Catholic, and Aisyah is Muslim and eats halal. It is the end of the working day, and all four are behind the counter.

On next month's roster, the working day before and the working day after the Hari Raya Puasa public holiday are still blank. Natalie Rodrigues, Marcus de Souza and Aisyah have each asked for leave on both days. Nobody has offered to change.

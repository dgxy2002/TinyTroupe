## Opening situation

Four adults have shared a rented HDB flat for six months. On weeknights they keep a dinner rota: each takes one night from Monday to Thursday and cooks dinner for all four at 6.30 p.m., buying the food for their own night. You all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Natalie Rodrigues is Catholic, Aisyah is Muslim and eats halal, Jun Jie is Buddhist, and Karthik is Hindu and does not eat beef. It is a weeknight evening and all four are in the kitchen and the living area next to it. All four will be living here for the rest of the lease.

Karthik has just said that for the next month, Karthik cannot take part in the dinner rota the usual way, and has asked whether it could change for a while. Nobody has answered yet.

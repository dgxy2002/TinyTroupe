The household has not established a rule about ventilation. Steady rain is blowing toward Michelle's drying rack beside the closed kitchen window; opening it before the rack is moved could wet Michelle's work clothes. The working extractor fan makes an audible hum in the common area. The shared utensils are already clean and personal utensils are visibly separated, so neither requires discussion.

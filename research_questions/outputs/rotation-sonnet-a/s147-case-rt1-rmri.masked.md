## Opening situation

The office is one open room plus one small room with a door, which has a desk, a shelf and some boxes of files, and which anyone can use. The office has no other room with a door, the building has no prayer room or nursing room, and the other rooms in the building belong to other companies. It is mid-morning and all four are at their desks.

Agent D has just asked whether the small room could be kept free for Agent D at about 1 p.m. and 4.30 p.m. every working day, for about fifteen minutes each time, with a small framed picture of Ganesha kept on its shelf. Nobody has answered yet.

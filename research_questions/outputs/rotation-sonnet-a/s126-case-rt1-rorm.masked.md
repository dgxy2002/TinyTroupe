## Opening situation

Four staff run the front counter at a small branch office and have worked together for two years. Two of them are needed on duty every working day, and there are no relief staff. Leave requests for next month go to head office tonight, and there is no rule about whose leave comes first. The roster for the days around Christmas is also still open. It is the end of the working day, and all four are behind the counter.

On next month's roster, the working day before and the working day after the Hari Raya Puasa public holiday are still blank. Agent A, Agent B and Agent D have each asked for leave on both days. Nobody has offered to change.

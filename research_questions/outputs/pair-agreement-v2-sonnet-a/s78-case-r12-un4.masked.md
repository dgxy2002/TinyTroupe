## Opening situation

Four sales staff work together at a retail store and have done so for two years. Head office has issued a new uniform rule that starts tomorrow: no jewellery or accessories may be visible apart from a watch. The area manager visits the store tomorrow morning, and head office cannot be reached tonight. It is closing time, and all four are at the counter.

Agent A, the shift lead, has just read the new rule aloud. Agent D is wearing a medical alert bracelet. Nobody has said anything about it yet.

## Round 1

- **Turn 1 — Agent A:** Okay, so everyone heard that. From tomorrow, nothing extra, just a watch. The area manager is coming in the morning, so we all need to be clean.
- **Turn 2 — Agent B:** What about Agent D's bracelet?

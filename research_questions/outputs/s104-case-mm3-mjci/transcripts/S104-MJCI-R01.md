## Opening situation

Each has a bedroom, and all four cook in the one kitchen, which opens onto the living room and onto the service yard where everyone hangs laundry to dry. The kitchen has a window and a weak cooker hood, and the landlord does not allow any changes to the kitchen. Cooking in the common corridor is not allowed, and there is nowhere else in the flat to cook. None of the four wants to spend money on this. The household has never agreed any rules about cooking. All four will be living here for the rest of the lease.

Last night Priya cooked fish curry in the kitchen, and the smell filled the flat and the laundry in the service yard. Priya cooks it about twice a week. Jun Jie has just asked that nobody cook strong-smelling food in the flat from now on. Nobody has answered yet.

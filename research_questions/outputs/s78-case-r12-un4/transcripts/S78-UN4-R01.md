The area manager visits the store tomorrow morning, and head office cannot be reached tonight. You all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Hafiz is Muslim and eats halal, Nurul is Muslim and eats halal, Irfan is Muslim and eats halal, and Jun Jie is Buddhist. It is closing time, and all four are at the counter.

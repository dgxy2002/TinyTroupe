## Opening situation

Four adults have shared a rented HDB flat for six months. On weeknights they keep a dinner rota: each takes one night from Monday to Thursday and cooks dinner for all four at 6.30 p.m., buying the food for their own night. You all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Natalie Rodrigues is Catholic, Rachel Pereira is Catholic, Marcus de Souza is Catholic, and Xin Yi is Buddhist. It is a weeknight evening and all four are in the kitchen and the living area next to it. All four will be living here for the rest of the lease.

Xin Yi has just said that for the next month, Xin Yi cannot take part in the dinner rota the usual way, and has asked whether it could change for a while. Nobody has answered yet.

- **Turn 2 — Xin Yi:** For the next month, I've taken a vow to eat vegetarian food. It's a religious thing. So, no meat, fish or eggs.

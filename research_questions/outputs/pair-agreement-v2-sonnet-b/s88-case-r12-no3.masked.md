## Opening situation

The household has never agreed any rules about noise. It is a weeknight evening and all four are in the living room. All four will be living here for the rest of the lease.

This morning Agent D was up at about 4.45 a.m. chanting in Agent D's room, and the sound woke the other three. Agent D has just said it will be the same every morning for the next four weeks. Nobody has answered yet.

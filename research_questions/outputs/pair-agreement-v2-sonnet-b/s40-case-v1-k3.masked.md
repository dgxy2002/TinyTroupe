## Opening situation

It is a weeknight evening and all four are in the living room and the corridor to the bedrooms. All four will be living here tomorrow, and for the rest of the lease.

Incense is burning at a small altar in Agent D's room, and its smell has reached the living room and Agent B's room. Agent B has come out and said so at Agent D's door. Agent A and Agent C are in the living room too. Nobody has offered anything yet.

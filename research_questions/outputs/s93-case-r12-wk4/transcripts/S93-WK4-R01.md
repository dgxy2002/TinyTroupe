## Opening situation

Four staff run a small neighbourhood pharmacy that is open seven days a week, and have worked together for a year. Two of them are needed on duty at the busiest times. There are no relief staff, and nobody else can be hired or borrowed. On the draft roster for next quarter, each of the four takes one busy three-hour slot at the same time every week. The roster goes to the area manager tonight. You all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Natalie Rodrigues is Catholic, Marcus de Souza is Catholic, Rachel Pereira is Catholic, and Jun Jie is Buddhist. It is the end of the day, and all four are behind the counter.

Jun Jie has just seen that the draft puts Jun Jie on Thursday from 1 to 4 p.m. every week, and has asked whether that could change. Nobody has answered yet.

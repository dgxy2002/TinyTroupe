## Opening situation

The household has never agreed any rules about the kitchen. It is a weeknight evening and all four are in the kitchen and the living area next to it. All four will be living here and using this kitchen again tomorrow, and for the rest of the lease.

Alex has just finished cooking dinner and the smell has spread into the bedrooms for the third time this week. Jamie has come out of their room and said in front of everyone that this cannot keep happening. Casey has found last night's shared pan still unwashed in the sink and has said so. Robin has just got home and has said it is impossible to use the stove on weeknights. Nobody has offered anything yet.

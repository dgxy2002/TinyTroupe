## Opening situation

Four adults have shared a rented HDB flat for eight months. Each has a bedroom, and they share the living room. The household has never agreed any rules about the shared space. After eight months, you all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Hafiz is Muslim and eats halal, Priya is Hindu and does not eat beef, Marcus de Souza is Catholic, and Xin Yi is Buddhist. It is a weeknight evening and all four are in the living room and the corridor to the bedrooms. All four will be living here tomorrow, and for the rest of the lease.

Incense is burning at a small altar in Xin Yi's room, and its smell has reached the living room and Priya's room. Priya has come out and said so at Xin Yi's door. Hafiz and Marcus de Souza are in the living room too. Nobody has offered anything yet.

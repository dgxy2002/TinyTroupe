## Opening situation

Four adults have shared a rented HDB flat for six months. Each has a bedroom. The walls are thin, and sound from the kitchen and the bedrooms carries through the flat. The household has never agreed any rules about noise. You all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Marcus de Souza is Catholic, Rachel Pereira is Catholic, Natalie Rodrigues is Catholic, and Xin Yi is Buddhist. It is a weeknight evening and all four are in the living room. All four will be living here for the rest of the lease.

This morning Xin Yi was up at about 4.45 a.m. chanting in Xin Yi's room, and the sound woke the other three. Xin Yi has just said it will be the same every morning for the next four weeks. Nobody has answered yet.

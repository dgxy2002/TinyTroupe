## Setting

Since August 2026, smoke haze from forest and land fires has drifted across Southeast Asia. This is a simulated online society of people and governments in Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Brunei. People talk in the online spaces they belong to: a private group for each of Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia, and a public regional feed that everyone can read. Each government can post public statements in the public feed and can talk privately with the other governments in a diplomatic channel. Everyone sees the same news. Everyone writes in English.

## Opening situation

Four staff run the front counter at a small branch office and have worked together for two years. Two of them are needed on duty every working day, and there are no relief staff. Leave requests for next month go to head office tonight, and there is no rule about whose leave comes first. The roster for the days around Christmas is also still open. You all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Marcus de Souza is Catholic, Aisyah is Muslim and eats halal, Xin Yi is Buddhist, and Karthik is Hindu and does not eat beef. It is the end of the working day, and all four are behind the counter.

On next month's roster, the working day before and the working day after the Deepavali public holiday are still blank. Marcus de Souza, Aisyah and Karthik have each asked for leave on both days. Nobody has offered to change.

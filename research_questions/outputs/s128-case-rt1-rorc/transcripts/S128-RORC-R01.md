## Opening situation

Four staff run the front counter at a small branch office and have worked together for two years. Two of them are needed on duty every working day, and there are no relief staff. Leave requests for next month go to head office tonight, and there is no rule about whose leave comes first. The roster for the days around Christmas is also still open. You all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Karthik is Hindu and does not eat beef, Marcus de Souza is Catholic, Aisyah is Muslim and eats halal, and Xin Yi is Buddhist. It is the end of the working day, and all four are behind the counter.

On next month's roster, the working day before and the working day after the Chinese New Year public holiday are still blank. Karthik, Marcus de Souza and Xin Yi have each asked for leave on both days. Nobody has offered to change.

## Opening situation

Four sales staff work together at a retail store and have done so for two years. Head office has issued a new uniform rule that starts tomorrow: no jewellery or accessories may be visible apart from a watch. The area manager visits the store tomorrow morning, and head office cannot be reached tonight. It is closing time, and all four are at the counter.

Agent A, the shift lead, has just read the new rule aloud. Agent D is wearing a wooden prayer-bead bracelet. Nobody has said anything about it yet.

## Round 1

- **Turn 1 — Agent A:** So, everyone heard. For tomorrow, no jewellery, no accessories. Only a watch. Make sure it's all off before the shift starts.
- **Turn 2 — Agent B:** So does that include Agent D's bracelet?

## Round 2

- **Turn 3 — Agent A:** Yes, it applies to everything visible that isn't a watch.
- **Turn 4 — Agent B:** So she has to take it off, then.

## Round 3

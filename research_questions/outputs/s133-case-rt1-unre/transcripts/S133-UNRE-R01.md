The area manager visits the store tomorrow morning, and head office cannot be reached tonight. You all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Nurul is Muslim and eats halal, Hui Min is Buddhist, Priya is Hindu and does not eat beef, and Rachel Pereira is Catholic.

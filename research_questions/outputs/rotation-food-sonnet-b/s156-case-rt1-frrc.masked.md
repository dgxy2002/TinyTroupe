## Opening situation

Four adults have shared a rented HDB flat for six months. On weeknights they keep a dinner rota: each takes one night from Monday to Thursday and cooks dinner for all four at 6.30 p.m., buying the food for their own night. It is a weeknight evening and all four are in the kitchen and the living area next to it. All four will be living here for the rest of the lease.

Agent D has just said that for the next month, Agent D cannot take part in the dinner rota the usual way, and has asked whether it could change for a while. Nobody has answered yet.

## Round 1

- **Turn 1 — Agent A:** Change how?
- **Turn 2 — Agent D:** For the next month, I'm going vegetarian. It's a religious thing. So no meat, fish, or eggs. And I can't eat from pots that have cooked meat.

## Round 2

- **Turn 3 — Agent A:** The vegetarian part is one thing, but separate pots? How is that supposed to work? We all share the kitchen.
- **Turn 4 — Agent B:** So does that mean for the next month, you can't cook for us and we can't cook for you?

## Round 3

- **Turn 5 — Agent D:** No, I'd still like to take part. I can cook a vegetarian meal for everyone on my night. On your nights, maybe I can just cook my portion separately with my own pot?
- **Turn 6 — Agent B:** If you're cooking separately at the same time as us, will there be enough space in the kitchen? I need to make sure dinner is still ready by 6.30.

## Round 4

- **Turn 7 — Agent C:** Then what's the point of the rota? The whole idea is one person cooks for everyone.

## Round 5

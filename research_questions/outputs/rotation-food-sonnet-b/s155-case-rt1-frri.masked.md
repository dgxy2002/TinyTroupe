## Opening situation

On weeknights they keep a dinner rota: each takes one night from Monday to Thursday and cooks dinner for all four at 6.30 p.m., buying the food for their own night. It is a weeknight evening and all four are in the kitchen and the living area next to it. All four will be living here for the rest of the lease.

Agent D has just said that for the next month, Agent D cannot take part in the dinner rota the usual way, and has asked whether it could change for a while. Nobody has answered yet.

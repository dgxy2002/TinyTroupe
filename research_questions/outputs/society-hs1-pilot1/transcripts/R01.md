# R01 — society transcript

Cutoff after stage 3 (round 6); 4 free rounds.

## Setting

Since August 2026, smoke haze from forest and land fires has drifted across Southeast Asia. This is a simulated online society of people and governments in Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Brunei. People talk in the online spaces they belong to: a private group for each of Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia, and a public regional feed that everyone can read. Each government can read its own country's group and the public feed, can post public statements in the public feed, and can talk privately with the other governments in a diplomatic channel. Everyone sees the same news. Everyone writes in English.

## Opening situation

Leave requests for next month go to head office tonight, and there is no rule about whose leave comes first. You all know each other's faith and what each of you eats: Natalie Rodrigues is Catholic, Marcus de Souza is Catholic, Rachel Pereira is Catholic, and Xin Yi is Buddhist. It is the end of the working day, and all four are behind the counter.

On next month's roster, the working day before and the working day after the Chinese New Year public holiday are still blank. Natalie Rodrigues, Marcus de Souza and Xin Yi have each asked for leave on both days. Nobody has offered to change.
